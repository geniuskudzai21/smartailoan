Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a loan?
Navigate to "Apply for a Loan" from the sidebar, fill in your details, upload the required KYC documents, and submit. Your application will be reviewed and you'll receive a notification once processed.
What documents are required?
You need to upload a National ID, Payslip, Utility Bill, Insurance Documents, and Bank Statements. All documents must be clear and valid.
How long does approval take?
Most applications are processed within 24–48 hours. You will receive a notification once your application status is updated.
How do I track my repayment schedule?
Go to "Loan Repayment Status" in the sidebar to view all your active loans, repayment periods, and smart contract tracking details.
